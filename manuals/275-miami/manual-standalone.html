House Rules

A few simple guidelines to keep everyone happy.

Our home is your home. These small courtesies help us protect the space so future guests can enjoy it just as you did.

01

Quiet hours after 10 PM

Please keep outdoor music low and gatherings mellow after 10 PM to respect our neighbors.

02

No parties or events

This is a residential neighborhood, loud parties are not permitted at any time.

03

Smoking outdoors only

Please smoke exclusively in the outdoor patio, away from doorways and windows.

04

Guests on the reservation

Only registered guests may stay overnight. Day visitors are welcome, please let us know in advance.

05

Clean up after the grill

Propane, coals and food are not provided. Please clean the grill after each use.

06

Leave used towels in the laundry

On the day of departure, please place all used towels in the laundry room. Leave the beds, we'll handle the linens.

07

Treat the space with care

If anything breaks or malfunctions, contact us right away, we'll never charge for honest wear and tear.

08

Lock up when you leave

Please secure all doors and windows any time the home is unoccupied.
