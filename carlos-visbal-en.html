01 · Biography

A comprehensive view of the real estate business.

Throughout his career, he has built solid experience in the South Florida real estate market, with a special focus on Miami and Brickell.

He has taken part in residential, commercial and multifamily transactions, working with clients and investors both in the United States and in markets across Latin America and the Caribbean.

He currently serves as Managing Partner of First Way Realty, where he leads his real estate activity in Florida, guiding buyers, owners and investors in identifying, negotiating and marketing real estate opportunities.

His experience, however, goes far beyond traditional property sales. Carlos has built a comprehensive view of the real estate business, combining expertise in negotiation, strategic planning, project management, financing, budget management and investment opportunity development.

04 · Pre-construction

Pre-construction specialist with direct developer relationships.

Another area that is part of his career is pre-construction in Miami and South Florida.

Carlos works directly with developers and real estate projects, allowing him to guide buyers and investors interested in opportunities from the earliest stages of a development.

His pre-construction experience covers project analysis, location, value proposition, investment structure and asset potential, helping clients understand not only what they are buying, but also how that property can fit into a medium- and long-term real estate strategy.
